# I paid, but my invoice still shows Pending

<p class="vp-lead">Why a paid Linkrunner invoice can still show Pending or keep your dashboard restricted, and what to do about it.</p>

<Info>

**At a glance:** card, UPI and netbanking payments clear straight away. A bank transfer stays **Pending** until you submit its reference. Access comes back automatically once every overdue invoice is paid, and attribution never stops in the meantime.

</Info>

**Applies to:** invoices on the [billing page](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/billing). For how billing works, see [Billing overview](/billing/overview).

## Check these in order

### 1. Did you pay by bank transfer without submitting the UTR?

**Fix:** open the invoice, click **Pay Now** and submit the UTR. See [Pay by bank transfer](/billing/overview#pay-by-bank-transfer).

### 2. Did one transfer cover two invoices, or less than the full amount?

One transfer clears one invoice, in full. **Fix:** email support with the UTR and the invoice numbers so we can match it by hand.

### 3. Is an older invoice still overdue?

Paying the newest invoice doesn't lift a restriction while an older one is open. See [What happens when an invoice is overdue?](/billing/overview#what-happens-when-an-invoice-is-overdue).

### 4. Paid by card, UPI or netbanking and still Pending?

These clear as soon as the payment succeeds. **Fix:** refresh the billing page. If your bank shows the money taken and the invoice is still Pending, email support with the receipt.

### 5. A download link says it has expired, or the invoice is wrong?

See [Download an invoice or receipt](/billing/overview#download-an-invoice-or-receipt) and [If an invoice is wrong](/billing/overview#if-an-invoice-is-wrong).

## Confirm it's fixed

The invoice shows as paid on the billing page, you received a receipt by email, and the dashboard opens normally.

## Still stuck?

Email support with:

- Your account or company name
- The invoice number
- The payment method, date and UTR or payment receipt

Don't send card numbers or bank login details.
