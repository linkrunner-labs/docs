## Overview

Use this guide for native Android and iOS App campaigns. Receiving an event, importing a conversion action, and using it for campaign bidding are separate steps.

## Setup Process

Setting up Google Ads integration involves four main steps: connecting your Google Ads account, creating a link ID for your mobile app, mapping events in Linkrunner, and importing events for conversion tracking.

<Note>
    Running iOS App Campaigns? Google measures iOS installs via SKAdNetwork. After completing this guide, follow the
    [Google Ads SKAN Setup](/features/google-skan-setup) guide.
</Note>

## 1. Connect Your Google Ads Account

Start in the Linkrunner dashboard.

1. Go to [Integrations](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations) and click **Configure** on the **Google Ads** card

![All Integrations page with the Google Ads card](/images/google_ads/all-integrations-google-ads.png)

2. On the **Account Integration** tab, click **Add Google Account**

3. Sign in with a Google account that has access to your Google Ads account and approve the requested permissions

Once connected, your account appears in the **Google Ad Accounts** table with **Auth Status** showing **Connected**.

## 2. Create Link ID for Android/iOS App

To connect your mobile app with Google Ads, you'll need to create a link ID that enables data sharing between Linkrunner and Google Ads.

### Step 1: Access Third-party App Analytics

1. Go to your Google Ads dashboard
2. Navigate to **Tools** (side panel) → **Data Manager** → **Third-party app Analytics**
3. Or visit directly: [https://ads.google.com/aw/productlinks/thirdpartyappanalytics](https://ads.google.com/aw/productlinks/thirdpartyappanalytics) and click on **Create Link ID**

![Google Ads Dashboard - Third-party App Analytics](/images/google_ads/google-ads-dashboard-1.webp)

### Step 2: Add App Analytics Provider

1. In the Third-party app analytics section, click on the **"+ Create link ID"**
2. Select **"Other provider"** from the App analytics provider dropdown menu
3. Enter the following provider ID: `9936233049`
4. Select the platform (iOS or Android) and search for your app
5. Click on **"Create link ID"**

![Google Ads third-party app analytics form with Other provider selected](/images/google_ads/google-ads-dashboard-2.webp)

Google Ads then shows the generated link ID. Copy it.

![Google Ads confirmation dialog with the generated link ID and its copy button](/images/google_ads/google-ads-dashboard-3.webp)

<Note>Link IDs are per platform. Repeat Step 2 for your other platform if you ship both Android and iOS.</Note>

### Step 3: Configure in Linkrunner Dashboard

Now paste that link ID into Linkrunner.

1. Go to [Integrations → Google Ads](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/google-ads) in your Linkrunner dashboard
2. On the **Account Integration** tab, open the three-dot menu on your Google Ads account row

![Linkrunner Google Ad Accounts table with the three-dot menu on the account row highlighted](/images/google_ads/link-ids/accounts-table-menu.png)

3. Choose **Setup Link IDs**

![Account row menu open showing the Setup Link IDs option](/images/google_ads/link-ids/setup-link-ids-menu.png)

4. Paste the link ID into **Android Link ID** or **iOS Link ID**, matching the platform you selected in Google Ads
5. Click **Save Link IDs**

![Setup Link IDs dialog with the Android field, iOS field, and Save Link IDs button highlighted in order](/images/google_ads/link-ids/setup-link-ids-modal.png)

<Note>You only need one link ID to save, but add both if you ship on Android and iOS.</Note>

## 3. Set Up Event Mapping

Map your app events to Google Ads events so Linkrunner can send postback conversions.

<Note>
    **Install** and **Sign up** are included by default as `first_open` and `sign_up`. You do not need to add mappings
    for these events.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open app event mapping">
    Go to [Integrations → Google Ads → Event Mapping](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/google-ads?tab=event_mapping) and open **App Event Mapping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your event">
    Click **Add Mapping**, select **Your Events**, and choose the matching **Google Ads Event**. Turn on **Send Revenue** only for revenue events, choose the **Currency**, then click **Save**.

    Match the Google event name exactly. `Signup` and `sign_up` are different events. A custom mapping to `Signup` does not use the default `sign_up` action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check event delivery">
    Saving a new or updated mapping automatically attempts to send a test event. You do not need to click the paper plane **Send event to ad network** action after every save. Use it for a manual retry if needed, or trigger the event in your app.

    A success message does not prove that Google received the event, discovered a conversion action, or imported it. Google processing time varies. Refresh the conversion actions after processing rather than adding duplicate mappings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. Manage Google conversion actions

Stay in **App Event Mapping** on [Google Ads → Event Mapping](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/google-ads?tab=event_mapping) and find **Google app conversion goals**. Linkrunner matches mapped events to native app conversion actions discovered by Google. Discovery identifies an action, not receipt of an individual event. You cannot create an arbitrary new native action here.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the account and platform">
    Choose your Google Ads account, then **Android** or **iOS**. Confirm that the account has a link ID for that platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the discovered action">
    ![Google app conversion goals showing the account, platform, and an enabled action using illustrative demo data](/images/google_ads/app-conversion-goals-overview.png)

    Find your mapped event and check its matching Google conversion action. If discovery is pending, confirm the exact mapped name, send an event using the existing test action if needed, then click **Refresh goals** after Google processes it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the goal editor">
    For a discovered action with status **HIDDEN**, click **Import** to open the editor. This does not save or enable the action yet. For an enabled action, click **Edit goal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the action and goal settings">
    ![Goal editor with category, Primary optimization, account-default settings, and Save changes for a demo action](/images/google_ads/app-conversion-goals-editor.png)

    Review **Goal category**, choose **Primary** or **Secondary** under **Action optimization**, and review **Account default goal**.

    For a hidden action, set **Import conversion action** to **Import and enable** if you want to import it, or **Keep hidden** to leave it unimported.

    The category and Primary or Secondary setting apply to the conversion action. Account-default changes apply to the account-level goal and can affect other actions and campaigns that use it, not just this event row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and save">
    ![Confirmation dialog explaining the account-wide impact before saving a demo conversion action](/images/google_ads/app-conversion-goals-confirm.png)

    Click **Save changes** to open the **Confirm Google Ads account changes** dialog. Review the affected account, action, and goal, then click **Confirm and save** only if the changes are correct.

    Importing enables the existing Google Ads action only after you confirm and the save succeeds. It does not create a new native action or select it for campaign bidding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Google can automatically enable app conversion actions as **Secondary**. These are for observation, not ordinary bidding optimization. If Google creates a new goal for an automatically enabled action, it does not make that goal an account default. See [Google's automatically enabled app conversion actions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17086025?hl=en).
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Primary** alone does not select an action for every campaign. Bidding also depends on the campaign's goal configuration. Review campaign-specific goal and action selection in Google Ads. Linkrunner does not manage campaign-specific selection here.
</Warning>

For the import behavior, see [Google's native app conversion action API guidance](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/conversions/categories#firebase_and_third-party_app_analytics).

## 5. Create Your First Campaign

With steps 1 to 4 complete, launch an App campaign in Google Ads. App campaigns need no tracking links. Attribution flows through the link ID, and campaigns appear in Linkrunner automatically as installs come in.


### Step 1: Start a New Campaign

1. Open [Google Ads](https://ads.google.com) and go to **Campaigns**
2. Click the **+** button and choose **New campaign**

![Google Ads Campaigns page with the create button and New campaign menu item highlighted](/images/google_ads/first-campaign/new-campaign-button.png)

3. Under **What's your campaign objective?**, select **App promotion** and click **Continue**

![Campaign objective screen with the App promotion card highlighted](/images/google_ads/first-campaign/objective-app-promotion.png)

### Step 2: Choose Subtype and Select Your App

1. Under **Select a campaign subtype**, choose the subtype that matches your goal. **App installs** is the right choice for most first campaigns.
2. Select your app's platform (**Android** or **iOS**)
3. Under **Look up your app**, search by app name, package name, or store URL and select your app
4. Enter a campaign name and click **Continue**

![Campaign subtype screen with App installs and the Android platform highlighted](/images/google_ads/first-campaign/subtype-app-installs.png)

<Note>
    Pick a different subtype when your goal is not new installs:

    - **App engagement**: re-engage users who already have your app with in-app actions. Google requires a minimum of
      50K installs.
    - **App pre-registration** (Android only): drive Play Store pre-registrations before your app launches.
</Note>

### Step 3: Set Locations and Languages

1. Under **Locations**, select the countries to run in
2. Under **Languages**, select the languages your customers speak
3. Click **Next**

![Campaign settings screen with the Locations and Languages sections highlighted](/images/google_ads/first-campaign/campaign-settings.png)

### Step 4: Add Ad Assets

App campaigns generate ads automatically from the assets you provide. Google mixes and matches them across Search, Play, YouTube, Display, and Discover.

1. Add up to 5 **Headlines** and 5 **Descriptions**
2. Upload images and videos if you have them
3. Click **Next**

![Ad group screen with the Headlines panel highlighted and the ad preview on the right](/images/google_ads/first-campaign/ad-assets.png)

### Step 5: Set Bidding and Budget

1. Under **What do you want to focus on?**, pick what Google should optimize for. You can choose either option; for your first campaign, choose **Install volume**.
2. Under **What kind of users do you want to target?**, pick either option. For this guide, we select **All users**.
3. Set your average daily **Budget**
4. Click **Next**, review the campaign, and click **Publish campaign**

![Bidding and budget screen with Install volume and All users highlighted](/images/google_ads/first-campaign/bidding-install-volume.png)

<Note>
    Bid on **In-app actions** only after your imported conversion actions have enough volume. Under **How do you want to
    track install volume?**, Google lists the download conversion actions imported from Linkrunner.
</Note>

### Step 6: Verify the Campaign in Linkrunner

Campaigns and spend sync automatically from your connected Google Ads account. Attribution rows appear after the campaign receives its first install.

1. Open the [**Campaigns** section](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard) in your Linkrunner dashboard
2. Click **All Channels** and select **Google** to filter your campaigns
3. Open the campaign to view installs, events, and revenue attributed to it

![Linkrunner Campaigns section with the channel filter open and Google selected](/images/google_ads/first-campaign/linkrunner-campaigns-google-filter.png)

<Note>
    Campaigns appear after the first attributed install, not immediately at launch. New campaigns can also take a few
    hours to exit Google's review and start serving.
</Note>

To open a specific screen after install, click **Edit** on the campaign row and set a **Deferred Deep Link**. Your app receives the URL from `getAttributionData()`. See [Deferred Deep Linking](/features/deferred-deep-linking).

## Important Notes

<Note>
    Google processes event receipt and conversion-action discovery separately. There is no fixed processing-time guarantee. Refresh the action list after processing and check the selected account, platform, and mapped event name if it remains pending.
</Note>

<Tip>
    Start with basic events like `first_open` to verify the integration is working before adding more complex conversion
    events.
</Tip>

<Note>
    Running App Campaigns for an iOS app? Set up [Google Integrated Conversion
    Measurement](/features/google-icm) so Google can attribute installs from users who declined App Tracking
    Transparency.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Campaign Not Showing in Linkrunner" description="Check attribution, the platform link ID, and your released SDK.">
    - Wait for the first attributed install before expecting attribution rows.
    - Confirm the platform link ID under [Integrations → Google Ads](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/google-ads?tab=integration).
    - Check that the campaign is active and serving impressions in Google Ads.
    - Confirm your released app includes the Linkrunner SDK at 100% rollout.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Events Not Showing Up" description="Check the exact mapped name and refresh after Google processes the event.">
    Confirm your app sends the event to Linkrunner and the correct platform link ID is saved. In [App Event Mapping](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/google-ads?tab=event_mapping), check the selected account, platform, and Google event name. `Signup` does not match `sign_up`.

    Use the existing test-event action if you have not sent the mapped event. A successful send is not proof of discovery or import. Allow Google to process it, then click **Refresh goals**. Do not create a duplicate action to bypass pending discovery. Contact support if it remains pending after these checks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Action is enabled but not used for bidding" description="Review Primary or Secondary, the account-default goal, and campaign settings.">
    Google may have automatically enabled the action as **Secondary**, with a new goal that is not an account default. Click **Edit goal** in Linkrunner and review **Action optimization** and **Account default goal**. Click **Save changes**, review the **Confirm Google Ads account changes** dialog, then click **Confirm and save**. Check campaign-specific goal selection in Google Ads. Importing an action does not select it for bidding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Re-enabling Previously Deleted Events" description="Find the removed action in Google Ads instead of creating a duplicate.">
    <span id="step-1-navigate-to-all-conversion-actions" />
    <span id="step-2-set-filter-to-show-all-conversions" />
    <span id="step-3-re-enable-the-deleted-event" />

    A removed action is different from a discovered **HIDDEN** action awaiting import. In [Google Ads conversions](https://ads.google.com/aw/conversions), open **View all conversion actions**, set the **Status** filter to **All**, find the removed action, and change its status to **Enabled**.

    Refresh App Event Mapping afterward. Re-enabling the action does not establish campaign bidding eligibility.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Link ID Creation Issues" description="Check your app's platform, store listing, and provider ID.">
    Select the correct platform, **Android** or **iOS**, and verify your app is published and searchable in its app store. Use provider ID `9936233049`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
