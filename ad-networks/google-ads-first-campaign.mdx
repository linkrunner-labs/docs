## Overview

Google App campaigns need no tracking links. Once your Google Ads integration is set up, attribution flows through the link ID and campaigns appear in Linkrunner automatically as installs come in.

This guide walks you through launching your first App campaign in Google Ads and verifying it in Linkrunner.

<Note>
    Running Search, Display, or Performance Max campaigns that drive installs through your website? Follow [Google Web
    to App](/ad-networks/google-web-to-app) instead. It has its own campaign creation flow.
</Note>

## Before you start

Complete these once. Without them, Google cannot attribute installs to Linkrunner and your campaign data will be incomplete.

1. **App live with the Linkrunner SDK.** Your app is released with the SDK integrated and verified using [Integration Testing](/testing/integration-testing).
2. **Google Ads account connected.** Follow [Google Ads](/ad-networks/google-ads) to connect your account in Linkrunner.
3. **Link ID created and saved.** Created in Google Ads with provider ID `9936233049` and saved in Linkrunner. See [Create Link ID](/ad-networks/google-ads#2-create-link-id-for-androidios-app).
4. **Events mapped and imported.** Map your events in Linkrunner, then import them as conversion actions in Google Ads. At minimum, import `first_open`. See [Event Mapping](/ad-networks/google-ads#3-set-up-event-mapping).
5. **iOS only: SKAN and ICM.** Complete [Google Ads SKAN Setup](/features/google-skan-setup) and [Google ICM](/features/google-icm) before running iOS campaigns.

<Warning>
    Google App campaigns do not accept custom tracking URLs. Attribution only works through the link ID, so do not
    launch the campaign until the link ID is saved in Linkrunner.
</Warning>

## 1. Start a new campaign

1. Open [Google Ads](https://ads.google.com) and go to **Campaigns**
2. Click **+ New campaign**
3. Under **What's your campaign objective?**, select **App promotion**
4. Under **Select a campaign subtype**, choose **App installs**
5. Select your app's platform (**Android** or **iOS**), search for your app, and select it
6. Click **Continue**

<Tip>
    Choose **App installs** for your first campaign. Use **App engagement** later for re-engaging existing users.
</Tip>

## 2. Set budget and bidding

1. Enter a campaign name
2. Set your **Locations** and **Languages**
3. Set a daily **Budget**
4. Under **Bidding**, choose what to focus on:
    - **Install volume** (recommended for your first campaign), targeting **All users**
    - **In-app actions** only after your imported conversion actions (from [Event Mapping](/ad-networks/google-ads#3-set-up-event-mapping)) have enough volume

<Note>
    The conversion actions available for in-app action bidding are the ones you imported from Linkrunner via
    **Goals → Conversions**. If an event is missing, fire a test event from Linkrunner and import it first.
</Note>

## 3. Add ad assets

App campaigns generate ads automatically from the assets you provide. Google mixes and matches them across Search, Play, YouTube, and Display.

1. Enter an ad group name
2. Add up to 5 **Headlines** and 5 **Descriptions**
3. Upload images and videos if you have them
4. Click **Next**, review the campaign, and click **Publish campaign**

## 4. Verify the campaign in Linkrunner

Campaigns and spend sync automatically from your connected Google Ads account. Attribution rows appear after the campaign receives its first install.

1. Go to the [Linkrunner Dashboard](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io) and open the **Campaigns** section
2. Filter by **Google** to see your campaign
3. Open the campaign to view installs, events, and revenue attributed to it

<Note>
    Campaigns appear after the first attributed install, not immediately at launch. New campaigns can also take a few
    hours to exit Google's review and start serving.
</Note>

### Set a deferred deep link (optional)

To open a specific screen after install, set a deferred deep link on the campaign in Linkrunner:

1. In the **Campaigns** section, click **Edit** on the campaign row
2. Enter your destination in the **Deferred Deep Link** field, for example `https://get.example.com/product/123`
3. Click **Save**

Your app receives the URL from `getAttributionData()`. See [Deferred Deep Linking](/features/deferred-deep-linking).

## Troubleshooting

### Campaign not showing in Linkrunner

- Wait for the first install. Campaigns appear only after at least one install is attributed.
- Confirm the link ID is saved for the right platform under [Integrations → Google Ads](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/google-ads?p_id=89).
- Confirm the campaign is active and serving impressions in Google Ads.
- Confirm your released app build includes the Linkrunner SDK at 100% rollout.

### Conversions not showing in Google Ads

- Events can take up to **6 hours** to appear after Linkrunner sends them, and up to **12 hours** after link ID creation.
- Verify the event is mapped under [Event Mapping](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/google-ads?tab=event_mapping) and imported under **Goals → Conversions** in Google Ads.

### iOS numbers look low

- Google measures iOS installs through SKAdNetwork. Check the [SKAN Dashboard](/features/skan-dashboard/overview) and review [iOS Campaign Data Limitations](/features/ios-campaign-data-limitations).

For campaign counts that do not match between Google Ads and Linkrunner, see [Google Ads FAQ and Discrepancies](/ad-networks/google-ads-discrepancies).
