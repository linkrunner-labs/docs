## Overview

Meta App Promotion campaigns need no tracking links. Once your Meta integration is set up, Linkrunner attributes installs through the Meta install referrer (Android) and SKAdNetwork (iOS), and campaigns appear in Linkrunner automatically as installs come in.

This guide walks you through launching your first App Promotion campaign in Meta Ads Manager and verifying it in Linkrunner.

<Note>
    Running website campaigns (Sales, Traffic) that send users to your app through a landing page? Follow [Meta Web to
    App](/ad-networks/meta-web-to-app) instead.
</Note>

## Before you start

Complete these once. Without them, installs from your campaign cannot be attributed.

1. **App live with the Linkrunner SDK.** Your app is released with the SDK integrated and verified using [Integration Testing](/testing/integration-testing).
2. **Meta app integration configured.** App ID and Install Referrer Decryption Key saved in Linkrunner. See [Meta Ads](/ad-networks/meta-ads#1-configure-meta-app-integration).
3. **Ad account connected and CAPI configured.** So Linkrunner can sync campaigns and send events to Meta. See [Connect Meta Ad Account](/ad-networks/meta-ads#step-4-connect-meta-ad-account).
4. **Events mapped.** So Meta receives your conversion events for optimization. See [Event Mapping](/ad-networks/meta-ads#2-set-up-event-mapping).
5. **App registered with Meta.** Your app must be set up in [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) with its store listing URL, and linked to the ad account you will run campaigns from.
6. **iOS only: SKAN.** Complete [Meta SKAN Setup](/features/meta-skan-setup) before running iOS campaigns.

## 1. Start a new campaign

1. Open [Meta Ads Manager](https://business.facebook.com/adsmanager) and select the ad account you connected to Linkrunner
2. Click **+ Create**
3. Select the **App promotion** objective and click **Continue**
4. If Meta asks how to create the campaign, either setup works. **Manual app promotion campaign** gives you full control over audience and placements; Advantage+ automates them. Linkrunner attributes both the same way.
5. Enter a campaign name and click **Next**

## 2. Configure the ad set

1. Under **App**, select the app store (**Google Play** or **App Store**) and your app

<Note>
    If your app is not listed, it is not registered with Meta or not linked to this ad account. Complete step 5 of
    [Before you start](#before-you-start).
</Note>

2. Set your **Budget & schedule**. Keep the budget low for the first campaign.
3. Define your **Audience** (locations, age, interests)
4. Leave **Advantage+ placements** on unless you have a reason not to
5. Click **Next**

## 3. Create the ad

1. Choose the Facebook Page and Instagram account for the ad's **Identity**
2. Upload your creative and write the primary text and headline
3. In **Destination**, choose **App**
4. To open a specific screen after install, add your link in **Deferred deep link**. Linkrunner syncs this value from Meta and returns it from `getAttributionData()`. See [Deferred Deep Linking](/ad-networks/meta-ads#5-deferred-deep-linking).

<img src="/images/meta/meta-app-promotion-destination.png" alt="Meta App Promotion Destination section with Deferred deep link and Custom store listing fields" />

<Warning>
    Do not use a Play Store or App Store URL as the deferred deep link. Meta handles the store destination separately.
</Warning>

5. Click **Publish**

## 4. Verify the campaign in Linkrunner

Campaigns and spend sync automatically from your connected ad account. Attribution rows appear after the campaign receives its first install.

1. Go to the [Linkrunner Dashboard](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io) and open the **Campaigns** section
2. Filter by **Meta** to see your campaign
3. Open the campaign to view installs, events, and revenue attributed to it, broken down by ad set and ad creative

<Note>
    Campaign, ad set, and ad creative rows appear after they receive at least one install, not immediately at launch.
    New campaigns can also take a few hours to exit Meta's review and start delivering.
</Note>

<Tip>
    On Android, enable [Meta view-through attribution](/ad-networks/meta-ads#6-enable-meta-view-through-attribution-android)
    to also attribute installs from users who saw your ad but did not click it.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

### Campaign not showing in Linkrunner

- Wait for the first install. Campaigns appear only after at least one install is attributed.
- Confirm the Install Referrer Decryption Key is saved under [Meta App Integration](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/integrations/meta-ads?tab=integration).
- Confirm the campaign is active and delivering in Meta Ads Manager.
- Confirm your released app build includes the Linkrunner SDK at 100% rollout.

### Events not showing in Meta Events Manager

- Events can take up to **1 hour** to appear after Linkrunner sends them.
- Verify your event mappings and fire a test event with the paper plane **Send to ad network** icon. See [Event Mapping](/ad-networks/meta-ads#2-set-up-event-mapping).

### iOS numbers look low

- Meta measures iOS installs through SKAdNetwork. Check the [SKAN Dashboard](/features/skan-dashboard/overview) and review [iOS Campaign Data Limitations](/features/ios-campaign-data-limitations).

For campaign counts that do not match between Meta Ads Manager and Linkrunner, see [Meta Ads FAQ and Discrepancies](/ad-networks/meta-ads-discrepancies).
