Invite `access@linkrunner.io` with **Basic** business portfolio access and **View performance** on the affected ad account. Add app or dataset permissions only when the support team needs them for your issue.

You need full control of the business portfolio to invite people. Select the portfolio that contains the affected assets before you start. Complete Meta's two-factor verification if prompted.

## 1. Open People settings

Open [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/), select your business portfolio, then go to **Settings → Users → People → Invite people**.

![Meta Settings with Users, People, and the Invite people button](/images/support-access/meta-people.webp)

## 2. Enter the support email

Enter `access@linkrunner.io` and confirm the address in the field. You can enable **Temporary access** if you want access to expire. Click **Next**.

![Meta invitation email step with the address redacted and Temporary access available](/images/support-access/meta-invite-email.webp)

## 3. Keep Basic access

Under **Give access to the business portfolio**, keep **Partial access → Basic**. Leave **Full access → Manage** off, then click **Next**.

![Meta business portfolio permissions with Basic access enabled and Full access Manage disabled](/images/support-access/meta-basic-access.webp)

## 4. Share the affected ad account

Under **Assign business assets**, select **Ad accounts**, choose the affected account, and enable **View performance**. Leave the campaign-management and full-access options off. Click **Next**.

![Meta ad account assignment with View performance enabled](/images/support-access/meta-ad-view-performance.webp)

On **Review invitation**, check the email address, **Basic** portfolio access, and **View performance** for the correct ad account. Click **Invite with 1 asset**, or the button showing your selected asset count.

![Meta invitation review showing Basic portfolio access and View performance for one ad account](/images/support-access/meta-review.webp)

Tell the support team which portfolio and ad account you shared. Access becomes available after Linkrunner accepts the invitation. You can check the person and their assigned assets under **Users → People**.

## Share an app or dataset when needed

Ad account access does not automatically grant access to your app or dataset. After Linkrunner joins the portfolio, use the relevant asset's **Assign people** button and select the person associated with `access@linkrunner.io`.

### App analytics

Go to **Settings → Accounts → Apps**, select your app, and click **Assign people**. Select Linkrunner's support user, enable **View insights**, then click **Assign**.

Meta also enables **Test app** with this selection. Leave **Develop app** and **Manage app** off.

![Meta app permissions with View insights and its included Test app permission enabled](/images/support-access/meta-app-view-insights.webp)

### Dataset or pixel events

Go to **Settings → Data sources → Datasets and pixels**, select the affected dataset or pixel, and click **Assign people**. Select Linkrunner's support user, enable **Use events dataset**, and click **Assign**. Leave **Manage events dataset** off.

This permission includes viewing data and analytics, and using the dataset for conversion ads. Meta does not offer a separate view-only option in this dialog.

![Meta dataset permissions with Use events dataset enabled and Manage events dataset disabled](/images/support-access/meta-dataset-use-events.webp)

## Remove access after support

Go to **Settings → Users → People**, find the person associated with `access@linkrunner.io`, and choose **More → Remove from business portfolio**. Confirm the removal.

## Troubleshooting

- **Invite people is disabled:** Ask someone with full control of the portfolio to send the invitation.
- **Meta says the person is already invited:** Find the existing entry under **People** and review its assigned assets instead of sending another invitation.
- **An asset is missing:** Check that you selected the portfolio that owns or has access to that asset.
- **Apps says a developer account is needed:** Contact Linkrunner support so they can complete the developer-account requirement before you assign the app.

For Google Ads, use the [Google support access guide](/ad-networks/google-support-access).

**Need help?** Contact [support@linkrunner.io](mailto:support@linkrunner.io).
