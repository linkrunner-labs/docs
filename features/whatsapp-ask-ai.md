# Ask Linkrunner AI on WhatsApp

<p class="vp-lead">Message Linkrunner AI on WhatsApp to ask about your installs, campaigns, spend and ROAS, or tag it in your team's WhatsApp group with Linkrunner.</p>

Get the same answers as Ask AI in the dashboard without opening it. Send a question from your phone, for example while you are in a meeting or away from your desk, and Linkrunner AI replies in the same chat.

You can use it in two ways:

- **Direct chat.** Message Linkrunner AI yourself. This is the main way to use it, and you connect your number once.
- **Your team's WhatsApp group with Linkrunner.** Tag Linkrunner AI in the group your team already shares with us, and everyone in the group sees the answer.

## Connect your number

Connect your WhatsApp number once. It takes about 10 seconds. Your number is shown in the dashboard when you connect.

<Steps>

<Step title="Start in the dashboard">

Open **Settings → MCP → WhatsApp** in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/settings/mcp) and tap **Connect WhatsApp**. If you message Linkrunner AI first, it sends you a link to the same screen.

![The Linkrunner on WhatsApp section in Settings, MCP, with the Connect WhatsApp button](/images/whatsapp-ask-ai/settings-not-connected.webp)

</Step>

<Step title="Send the message">

WhatsApp opens with a message already typed. On a computer, scan the QR code with your phone's camera instead. Press send. You can also type the 4-digit code the dashboard shows and send it to Linkrunner AI yourself.

![The Connect WhatsApp window with a QR code, the 4-digit code and a Copy code button](/images/whatsapp-ask-ai/connect-code.webp)

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm it is your number">

Back in the dashboard, you are asked **Is this your WhatsApp number?** Tap **Yes, connect**.

![The Is this your WhatsApp number? question with Not my number and Yes, connect buttons](/images/whatsapp-ask-ai/confirm-number.webp)

</Step>

<Step title="Start asking">

Linkrunner AI replies on WhatsApp that you are connected, with a few example questions.

![The WhatsApp section after connecting, with the connected number, the project for direct messages and example questions](/images/whatsapp-ask-ai/settings-connected.webp)

</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>

The confirmation step keeps your account safe. If someone else got hold of your code and sent it from their phone, they could not connect their number to your account without you tapping **Yes, connect**. The code works for 15 minutes and only once.

</Note>

## Ask a question

Message Linkrunner AI the way you would message a teammate. For example:

- How many installs did we get yesterday?
- Which campaigns performed best in the last 7 days?
- Why did installs drop compared with last week?
- What was our cost per install by network this week?

Linkrunner AI reacts to your message with 👀 as soon as it starts working, replies with the answer, and then adds ✅ to your message.

Tables are laid out to fit a phone, charts are shown as their numbers, and long answers are split into more than one message.

To tell us whether an answer helped, react to it with 👍 or 👎. This feedback is the same as the thumbs in the dashboard.

### Commands

Send one of these words on its own.

| Send | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `switch` | Linkrunner AI lists your projects. Reply with the number of the project you want answers about. |
| `new` | Starts a fresh chat, so earlier questions are not used as context. |
| `help` | Shows what you can ask. |

If you have more than one project, direct chats answer about the project you chose. Your chat also starts fresh on its own after 2 quiet hours.

## Use it in your team's WhatsApp group

If your team has a WhatsApp group with Linkrunner, Linkrunner AI can answer there too.

1. The Linkrunner team adds the Linkrunner AI number to your group and turns it on for your project. Ask your Linkrunner contact to set this up.
2. In the group, tag **Linkrunner AI** with your question, or reply to one of its answers to follow up.

Everyone in the group sees the answers, so people can build on each other's questions. Answers in a group cover only the project the group is set up for, and never personal chats or memory.

You also need to have connected your number first. If you have not, Linkrunner AI replies with the link to do it.

## Who can ask

- You must be an admin or a member of the project.
- Agency, affiliate and finance views cannot ask. Those accounts are not given access to the project's Ask AI.
- Ask AI must be turned on for the project.
- Questions use AI credit in the same way as Ask AI in the dashboard.

## Privacy and safety

- **Read-only.** Linkrunner AI reads your data and answers. It never changes anything. To change a campaign or setting, use the dashboard.
- **Your own access.** Every question runs as your Linkrunner account, so you can only get answers about projects you can already open.
- **Codes in groups are cancelled.** If the connect message is posted in a WhatsApp group, Linkrunner AI cancels it and asks you to get a new one, because everyone in the group could see it. Send your code in a direct chat only.
- **Numbers expire.** A number that is not used for 90 days needs to be connected again.
- **WhatsApp number privacy.** If you have number privacy turned on in WhatsApp, WhatsApp hides your number from us and you cannot connect yet. Turn it off for the chat with Linkrunner AI, or ask from your team's group with us.

## Disconnect or change your project

Open **Settings → MCP → WhatsApp** in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/settings/mcp).

- To change which project direct chats answer about, pick another project in the list, or send `switch` in the chat.
- To remove your number, tap **Disconnect**.

## Troubleshooting

**"I'm not set up in this group yet."**
The Linkrunner team has not turned on Linkrunner AI for this group. Ask your Linkrunner contact to turn it on for your project. Until then you can message Linkrunner AI directly.

**"That code has expired or was already used."**
Codes work for 15 minutes and only once. Tap **Connect WhatsApp** in **Settings → MCP → WhatsApp** to get a new one.

**"Too many codes tried."**
Wait 10 minutes, then use a new code from the dashboard.

**"You need admin or member access."**
Linkrunner AI replies that none of your projects can use it. Ask a project admin to make you an admin or member, and check that Ask AI is on for the project.

**No reply at all.**
Check that Ask AI is switched on for the project, and that the project has AI credit left. If both are fine and there is still no reply after a few minutes, contact support.
