Send a Linkrunner cohort to Google Ads or Meta as a custom audience for remarketing.

There are two ways to do this:

| | [Automatic sync](#automatic-sync) | [Manual upload](#manual-upload) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | Once, per cohort | Every time |
| Stays current | Yes | No — a snapshot of the day you exported |
| Removes people who no longer match | Yes | No |
| Works with | Google Ads, Meta | Google Ads, Meta |

<Note>
  A cohort is a **live rule**, not a fixed list of people. It is recalculated every
  time it is read, so a CSV you exported last week describes who matched last week.
  Automatic sync is what keeps the ad-network audience matching the rule.
</Note>

## Automatic sync

Map a cohort to an audience once, and Linkrunner keeps that audience matching the
cohort — adding people who start matching and removing people who stop.

### Before you start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the ad account">
    Go to **Integrations** and connect Google Ads or Meta for the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the audience in the ad platform">
    Linkrunner writes into an audience you already own; it does not create one for you.

    - **Google Ads** — create a **Customer list** in Audience manager and leave it empty.
    - **Meta** — create a **Custom Audience** with **Customer list** as the source.

    <Warning>
      On Google this must be a **Customer list**. Audiences that Google builds for you
      from rules — for example "All Converters" — cannot receive uploads, and a sync
      pointed at one will always fail.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set up the sync

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the cohort">
    Go to **Users → Cohorts**, click the cohort, and scroll to **Ad network sync**.

    {/* screenshot: Ad network sync panel, empty state */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a destination">
    Choose the **ad network** and **ad account**, then paste the audience ID:

    - **Google Ads** — the *Customer Match list ID*. Find it in **Tools → Shared library →
      Audience manager → Your data segments**; it is the numeric ID in the segment list,
      and also appears in the URL as `userListId=`.
    - **Meta** — the *Custom Audience ID*. Find it in the ID column in **Ads Manager → Audiences**.

    Set **Default country** if your stored phone numbers have no country code — see
    [Match rate](#match-rate) below.

    {/* screenshot: add destination form filled in */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the first sync">
    Click **Sync now**. The status chip shows what happened.

    {/* screenshot: destination row with status chip and drop stats */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Status

| Chip | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Never synced** | Mapped, but nothing has been pushed yet |
| **Syncing…** | A run is in progress |
| **Up to date** | Last run succeeded |
| **Cohort unchanged** | Nobody joined or left since the last run, so nothing was re-uploaded |
| **Last sync failed** | The run failed; the reason is shown underneath |
| **Paused** | Syncing is off until you resume it |
| **Reconnect ad account** | The ad account's authorisation expired — reconnect it in Integrations |

Under the chip, Linkrunner shows how many cohort members were not uploaded and why —
for example `1,204 of 5,000 not uploaded — 900 with no identifier, 304 invalid phone`.

### How long it takes to reflect

The sync itself finishes in seconds. The wait is on the ad platform's side.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Google Ads">
    Google accepts the upload immediately but applies it asynchronously. Allow **up to 48 hours**
    for membership to appear.

    Google also hides audiences that are too small. Until a list has roughly **100 matched
    members it cannot be used for Display**, and around **1,000** for Search and YouTube. Below
    those thresholds the audience shows a size of **0** even though the upload succeeded — this
    is the most common reason a working sync looks like it did nothing.

    <Warning>
      **Set the membership duration deliberately.** People are removed from a Google audience by
      *expiring*, not by being deleted: each sync refreshes everyone who still matches, so anyone
      who stops matching simply stops being refreshed and drops out when their membership ends.

      If membership duration is left at its 540-day maximum, effectively nobody is ever removed.
      Set it close to your sync frequency — 30 days is a reasonable default.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Meta">
    Meta usually reflects a replaced audience within an hour, though it can take longer for
    large audiences. Meta also needs roughly **1,000 matched people** before an audience can
    be targeted.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Match rate

Only people carrying an identifier the ad platform can match are uploaded. Before syncing,
the cohort preview shows how much of the cohort is matchable, broken down by email, phone,
GAID and IDFA.

A few things decide that number:

- **Email and phone are hashed** with SHA-256 before they leave Linkrunner. Advertising IDs
  (GAID, IDFA) are sent unhashed, which is what both platforms require.
- **Phone numbers need a country code.** A number stored as `9876543210` cannot be matched,
  because there is no way to tell which country it belongs to. Setting **Default country** on
  the destination fixes those; without it they are dropped rather than guessed at.
- **People who opted out of ad tracking are never uploaded**, even if the rest of their record
  would match.
- **People with no identifier at all are skipped.** A cohort of 15,000 users where only 60 have
  an email or phone on file will upload 60 people.

### Pausing and removing

**Pause** stops scheduled syncs while leaving the mapping in place — the audience keeps whoever
it has. **Remove** deletes the mapping only; the audience in the ad platform is left untouched.

## Manual upload

Use this for a one-off audience, or when you would rather not connect the ad account.

<Note>
  A manual upload is a snapshot. It does not update as the cohort changes, and people who stop
  matching are not removed — uploading a fresh file only ever adds to the audience.
</Note>

### Google Ads

#### 1. Prepare your data

- Create a segment in Linkrunner from Audience Cohort and download the CSV

#### 2. Upload to Google Ads

##### Navigate to Audience Manager

1. In your Google Ads account, click the **Tools** icon (the wrench) in the left menu
2. Under the "Shared library" section, click **Audience manager**

<img src="/images/google_ads/custom_audience/1.png" alt="Google Ads Audience Manager" />

##### Create a New Customer List

1. Click the blue **+** button

<img src="/images/google_ads/custom_audience/2.png" alt="Click the plus button" />

2. Select **Customer list** from the drop-down menu

<img src="/images/google_ads/custom_audience/3.png" alt="Select Customer list" />

3. Select "Upload a file manually" and click **Continue**

<img src="/images/google_ads/custom_audience/4.png" alt="Upload a file manually" />

##### Configure the List

- **Segment Name**: Give your audience a clear, recognizable name (e.g., "Newsletter Subscribers Q1")
- **Data Type**: Choose "Upload emails, phones, and/or mailing addresses"
- **Data to upload**: Attach your prepared CSV file
- **Membership Duration**: Choose how long you want a user to stay on this list. You can set a specific number of days or choose "No expiration" (the maximum is 540 days)

<img src="/images/google_ads/custom_audience/5.png" alt="Configure the list" />

Click on **"Save and continue"** to create the customer list.

### Meta

#### 1. Prepare your data

- Create a segment in Linkrunner from Audience Cohort and download the CSV

#### 2. Upload to Meta Ads

##### Navigate to the Audiences Dashboard

1. Open Meta Ads Manager
2. Click the **All Tools** menu (the three horizontal lines or "hamburger" icon) on the left sidebar
3. Select **Audiences**

<img src="/images/meta/custom_audience/1.png" alt="Meta Audiences Dashboard" />

##### Create a Custom Audience

1. Click the blue **Create Audience** dropdown button
2. Select **Custom Audience**

<img src="/images/meta/custom_audience/2.png" alt="Create Custom Audience" />

##### Choose Your Source

1. Under "Your sources," select **Customer list** and click **Next**

<img src="/images/meta/custom_audience/3.png" alt="Select Customer list" />

2. **Upload File**: Drag and drop your CSV or TXT file, or click to upload it

<img src="/images/meta/custom_audience/4.png" alt="Upload file" />

##### Set Up Your List

1. **Customer Value**: Meta will ask if your list includes a column for customer value. Select **No**, then click **Next**
2. **Name Your Audience**: Give it a clear, descriptive name (e.g., "Purchasers - March 2026") and an optional description. Click **Next**

##### Map Your Identifiers

1. Meta will preview your data and attempt to map your columns to its accepted identifiers
2. **Action Needed**: Review the "Mapped" and "Unmapped" tabs. If a column is unmapped, manually select the correct identifier from the dropdown menu, or tell Meta to ignore that column

<img src="/images/meta/custom_audience/5.png" alt="Map Your Identifiers" />
3. Click **Import and Create** to create the audience.
