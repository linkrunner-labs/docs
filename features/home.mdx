**Home** is the first page you see in the dashboard. It puts your project's key numbers on one page: headline totals, trends over time, your top campaigns, ad sets and creatives, and breakdowns by channel, device and country.

Open it at [dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/home](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/home). Every member of the project can see it.

## Pick a date range

Use the quick ranges next to the greeting: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **7d**, **30d**, or **90d**. Click the calendar to choose your own dates. Home opens on the last 30 days, and **Clear** takes you back to it.

## Filter by channel

Use the channel filter at the top right to show one or more channels, such as Meta Ads or Google Ads. The filter applies to every widget on the page. A widget can also have its own channel filter.

## Personalize the layout

Click **Personalize** to change what Home shows. While personalizing you can:

- Drag widgets to rearrange them, and resize them from the corner.
- Hide widgets you do not need, and show hidden ones again.
- Duplicate a widget and set up the copy differently, for example a second trend chart for one channel.
- Change what a widget shows, such as its metrics and chart type.

Click **Done** to keep your changes. **Reset full layout** puts every widget, filter and chart back to the default.

<Note>
  Your layout is saved in your browser, for you and this project only. It does not change Home for your teammates, and it does not follow you to another browser or computer.
</Note>

<Info>
  Rearranging and resizing need a screen at least 1280 pixels wide. On smaller screens the **Personalize** button is turned off.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Home is empty and says every widget is hidden">
    Click **Customize** to show your widgets again, or use **Reset full layout** while personalizing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A widget asks me to connect an ad account">
    Spend, cost and ROAS widgets need the matching ad account connected. Use the button on the widget to connect it, or hide the widget.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My teammate sees a different layout">
    Each person's layout is their own, and is saved per browser. Only the data is shared.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
