**Change History** lists every change made to your project's setup: settings, team members, integrations, postbacks, attribution settings, fraud rules, links and domains, partners, campaigns, and billing. Use it to answer "who changed this, and when?".

Open it from **Settings → Change History**, or go to [dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/settings/change-history](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/settings/change-history).

## What each entry shows

Entries are grouped by day, newest first, in your project's time zone. Each entry shows:

- **What changed**, with the old and new value for each field. Long values are shortened, and an entry that changed more than three fields shows the first three with a button to see the rest.
- **Who changed it** (hover over the name to see their email): a teammate, a former teammate, the Linkrunner team, an API key, or an AI assistant connected through [MCP](/features/mcp). Changes made by an agency or affiliate partner show the partner's name.
- **When**: hover over the time to see the exact date.

Secret values, such as keys and tokens, are never shown. The entry only says that they changed.

## Find a change

- **Search** matches names, people, partners and values across the whole history, for example a campaign name, a teammate's name, or a URL.
- **All areas** narrows the list to one area, such as **Postbacks** or **Fraud control**.

Scroll down and click **Load older changes** to go further back.

## Who can see it

Project **Admins** and **Members** can open Change History. Influencers and partners cannot. Billing changes are shown to Admins only.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot find Change History in Settings">
    It is the last item in the Settings menu. If it is missing, your role on this project does not include it. Ask a project Admin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page says there are no changes yet">
    Nothing has changed in that area since history started being recorded. Try **All areas**, or clear your search.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
