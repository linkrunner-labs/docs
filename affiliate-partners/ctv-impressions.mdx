Use this when your ads play on connected TVs (smart TVs, streaming sticks, set-top boxes, game consoles) and the app is installed on a phone in the same home. This is **CTV view-through attribution**.

Your **server** reports each impression with the TV's IP address and user agent. If a phone on that home network installs and opens the app within the view-through window, and nothing else can be credited, Linkrunner credits your impression. Your install postback then carries `{touch_type}` = `impression`.

- A TV ad has no click, and a TV's advertising ID never equals the phone's GAID or IDFA. The two share only the household's public IP address.
- If your impressions carry the phone's GAID or IDFA, use [S2S impressions](/affiliate-partners/s2s-impressions) instead. They match on the device ID and rank above CTV impressions.
- Linkrunner accepts AppsFlyer's CTV parameter names. See [Coming from AppsFlyer](#coming-from-appsflyer).

## Before you start

CTV is off by default. The advertiser turns on three switches for your partner account, on each app. Changes take up to 5 minutes to apply.

| Switch | Where the advertiser turns it on | Rejection while off |
|---|---|---|
| **S2S clicks and impressions** | **Integrations → Affiliate Partners → Configure** | `partner_not_enabled` |
| **Connected TV (CTV) impressions** | Under the S2S switch, same page ([steps](/affiliate-partners/turn-on-s2s-clicks#turn-on-ctv-impressions)) | `ctv_not_enabled` |
| **View-through**, with its window | **Settings → Attribution Windows** ([how](/features/attribution-windows#view-through-attribution)) | `view_through_disabled` |

Send your advertiser the [CTV attribution guide](/features/ctv-attribution).

## How it works

A CTV match is probabilistic. The same IP address suggests the same household, not the same person. Linkrunner credits a CTV impression only when all of these hold:

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Same IP address** | The phone installs from the public IP address the impression reported, in practice on the same home Wi-Fi as the TV. Installs over mobile data use the carrier's addresses and don't match. |
| **Android and iOS** | Installs on both platforms are credited. |
| **Everything else wins** | Any click that can be credited, from any source, and any [S2S impression](/affiliate-partners/s2s-impressions) matched on the device ID win over every CTV impression. |
| **Inside the window** | The install happens within the view-through window the advertiser set for you: 1 to 24 hours, 24 by default. Your `vt_lookback` can shorten it, never lengthen it. |
| **Latest impression** | If several impressions match, the most recent one inside its own window wins. Each impression is credited to at most one install. |
| **Not a shared address** | If more than four installs of the app from one IP address in a day have no click or device-ID impression credited, Linkrunner treats it as a shared network (an office, a public hotspot) and stops crediting CTV impressions to its installs. |

Postbacks work as for clicks. Your [postback templates](/affiliate-partners/postbacks), [event mapping](/affiliate-partners/event-mapping) and [postback logs](/affiliate-partners/postback-logs) apply unchanged.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your S2S token">
    If you already send [S2S clicks](/affiliate-partners/s2s-clicks) or [S2S impressions](/affiliate-partners/s2s-impressions), use the same token and skip this step.

    Otherwise, open [**Settings → S2S Credentials**](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/affiliate/settings?tab=s2s) and click **Generate token**. It starts with `lrs2s_` and is **shown only once**, so store it as a secret on your server. One token covers every app.

    ![S2S Credentials with the Generate token button](/images/affiliate-partners/ctv-s2s-credentials.webp)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the advertiser's app">
    Pick the advertiser's app in the switcher at the top left. Campaigns and the CTV setting are per app.

    <img src="/images/affiliate-partners/ctv-pick-customer-app.webp" alt="The app switcher open, with the advertiser's app under Projects and your partner account below it" width="250" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the campaign link">
    In **All Campaigns**, click **Create Link**, name the CTV campaign and click **Create Campaign**. Other options are in [Create a campaign link](/affiliate-partners/create-campaign-link).

    <img src="/images/affiliate-partners/ctv-create-campaign.webp" alt="Create Campaign with a CTV campaign name filled in" width="420" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the CTV impression URL">
    Once the advertiser turns on CTV for you, your dashboard builds this URL. Copy the **CTV impression URL** from any of these:

    - The **Campaign Created** screen, next to the **Template URL**.
    - The TV icon in the campaign table.
    - The campaign details panel.

    <img src="/images/ctv-attribution/ctv-campaign-created-url.webp" alt="Campaign Created with the Template URL, View-through URL and CTV impression URL" width="420" />

    It looks like this, plus your own `s3` and `s4` if your template has them:

    ```
    https://s2s.linkrunner.io/v1/ctv/impression?d=<link domain>&c=<campaign code>&tid={impression_id}&s2={campaign_id}&ip={device_ip}&ua={device_ua}&impression_time={timestamp_ms}
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the macros">
    Replace each macro in braces with your own value:

    - `ip` and `ua` are **required**. `ip` is the TV's public IP address, **not your ad server's**. With server-side ad insertion, use the `X-Device-IP` and `X-Device-User-Agent` values your ad server received.
    - `tid` is your ID for this impression. Make it unique per impression.
    - `impression_time` is when the impression happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fire it from your server when the ad plays">
    Call the URL with `GET` and your token in the `X-Linkrunner-Token` header. A `200` with `"status":"accepted"` means the impression is recorded. For other responses, see [retries](#retries).

    <Warning>
    Send each impression as it happens. An impression that arrives after the install can't be credited, and impressions older than 24 hours are rejected.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it end to end">
    Send one impression with the public IP address of a test home network. On a phone on that Wi-Fi, install and open the app within the window, without tapping any ad. Check [Postback logs](/affiliate-partners/postback-logs) for the install postback with your `tid` and `{touch_type}` = `impression`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Build the URL by hand">
  Start from the **Template URL** on the Campaign Created screen, for example `https://app.example.com/?c=OgWmhiSXhG&tid={click_id}&s2={campaign_id}`. Send its parameters to `https://s2s.linkrunner.io/v1/ctv/impression/<app_id>` and add `ip`, `ua` and `impression_time`:

  ```http
  GET https://s2s.linkrunner.io/v1/ctv/impression/com.example.app?c=OgWmhiSXhG&tid={impression_id}&ip={device_ip}&ua={device_ua}&impression_time={timestamp_ms}&source_platform={platform}
  X-Linkrunner-Token: <your_token>
  ```

  - `<app_id>` is the Android package name (`com.example.app`) or App Store ID (`id1234567890`). Either finds the same app, and both its Android and iOS installs are matched.
  - Or use the tracking link's domain: `https://s2s.linkrunner.io/v1/ctv/impression?d=app.example.com&c=...`. Use `d` if the advertiser's test and live apps share an app ID and the same campaign code.
</Accordion>

## See your results

In the campaign table, CTV installs count under **Installs** and **View Through Attribution**. Accepted impressions appear in the **Impressions** column, which is off by default. Turn it on once per app and it stays on:

1. In **All Campaigns**, click the columns icon at the right of the toolbar, below **Create Link**.

   ![The Edit Columns icon at the right of the campaign toolbar, below Create Link](/images/affiliate-partners/ctv-edit-columns-button.webp)
2. Tick **Impressions** and click **Save**. Keep **View Through Attribution** ticked as well.

   <img src="/images/affiliate-partners/ctv-edit-columns-impressions.webp" alt="Edit Columns with Impressions ticked and the Save button at the top" width="420" />

![Campaign table with Impressions, Installs and View Through Attribution for two CTV campaigns and one click campaign](/images/affiliate-partners/ctv-campaign-table.webp)

- A TV-only campaign has no clicks.
- A new campaign can show impressions before its first install. Installs count on the day they happen, so a household that saw the ad late at night can install the next day.
- A TV impression belongs to neither Android nor iOS. A view filtered to one platform hides CTV impressions but still shows that platform's credited installs. Clear the filter to see them.

## Authenticate your requests

Use the same partner token as [S2S impressions](/affiliate-partners/s2s-impressions#authenticate-your-requests). Send it in the `X-Linkrunner-Token` header, or as the `lr_token` query parameter if your ad server can only fire a URL. Without a valid token, the request is rejected with `401` and `missing_token` or `invalid_token`. The [API reference](/api-reference/ctv-impressions#examples) has cURL, Node.js and Python examples.

## Coming from AppsFlyer

You can reuse your AppsFlyer cross-platform CTV template. Change the host and `c`, add a unique `tid`, and add your [token](#authenticate-your-requests).

| AppsFlyer | Linkrunner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `impressions.onelink.me/<template_id>` | `s2s.linkrunner.io/v1/ctv/impression/<app_id>` | Linkrunner uses the Android package name or App Store ID, not a OneLink template |
| `c` (campaign name) | `c` (campaign code) | Use the `c` value from your Linkrunner tracking link, not a campaign name |
| | `tid` (or `clickid`) | Required. Send a unique ID per impression |
| `af_ip` | `ip` | Either name works. Required |
| `af_ua` | `ua` | Either name works. Required |
| `af_xplatform_vt_lookback` | `vt_lookback` | Works as is. `1h` to `24h`, or `1d`. Longer values are capped at 24 hours. `af_viewthrough_lookback` also works |
| `af_c_id`, `af_adset_id`, `af_ad_id`, `af_siteid`, `af_source_platform`, `af_source_device_category` | `partner_campaign_id`, `adset_id`, `ad_id`, `site_id`, `source_platform`, `device_category` | Either name works. Stored with the impression |
| `af_sub1`, `af_sub2`, `af_sub3` | `s2`, `s3`, `s4` | Either name works. Returned in `{s2}` to `{s4}` |
| `pid`, `af_xplatform`, `redirect` | Not needed | Ignored if present |

For example, the first template becomes the second, with your token in the `X-Linkrunner-Token` header:

```
https://impressions.onelink.me/AbCd?pid=xyz_int&c=summer&af_xplatform=true&af_xplatform_vt_lookback=24h&af_ip={ip}&af_ua={ua}&af_siteid={site}&redirect=false
https://s2s.linkrunner.io/v1/ctv/impression/com.example.app?c=OgWmhiSXhG&tid={impression_id}&af_xplatform=true&af_xplatform_vt_lookback=24h&af_ip={ip}&af_ua={ua}&af_siteid={site}
```

## Retries

Retrying is safe. The same `tid` for the same campaign always returns the same `impression_id` and is stored once.

- Retry `429` and `503`, after the `Retry-After` delay.
- Don't retry other `4xx` responses. Fix the request instead. The `reason` tells you what's wrong.

The [CTV Impression API reference](/api-reference/ctv-impressions) lists every parameter, response and rejection reason.

## Troubleshooting

| Response or symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| `missing_ip`, `invalid_ip`, `datacenter_ip`, `missing_ua` | Send the TV's own public IP address and user agent, not your ad server's. Private (`192.168.x.x`, `10.x.x.x`), carrier-grade NAT (`100.64.0.0/10`), loopback and cloud or hosting addresses are rejected. |
| `partner_not_enabled`, `ctv_not_enabled`, `view_through_disabled` | Ask the advertiser to turn on the missing switch. See [Before you start](#before-you-start). |
| Accepted, but no installs credited | Check each rule in [How it works](#how-it-works), and that the impression arrived before the install. |
