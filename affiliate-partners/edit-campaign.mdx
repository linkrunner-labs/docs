You can rename any campaign in your **All Campaigns** list. Fix a typo or a naming convention here instead of creating a new link.

## What you can change

| Setting | Who changes it |
|---|---|
| Campaign name | You |
| Deep link destination, link preview, redirects, domain, store listings | The advertiser's team |
| Ad channel | Nobody. It is fixed when the link is created. |
| Turning the link off, deleting the campaign | Nobody. A campaign credited to a partner can't be turned off or deleted. Stop sending traffic to it instead. |

Renaming changes only the name shown in the dashboard. Your link, its `c` value, your macros and your postbacks stay the same, and past clicks and installs stay on the campaign.

## Rename a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    Sign in to [Linkrunner Dashboard](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io), pick the advertiser project, open **All Campaigns** and click the campaign row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    **Edit** is at the top right of the campaign panel.

    ![The campaign panel with the Edit button at the top right](/images/affiliate-partners/edit-campaign-open.webp)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new name">
    Change **Campaign Name** and click **Update**. You see **Campaign updated successfully**, and the new name shows in **All Campaigns**.

    ![The Edit campaign form for an affiliate, with only the campaign name](/images/affiliate-partners/edit-campaign-name.webp)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion
    title="You need to change the deep link, redirects or another setting"
    description="Only the advertiser's team can change these."
  >
    Ask the advertiser's project admin to make the change. They edit the same campaign from their own dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="&quot;You can't make this change&quot;"
    description="The campaign is no longer credited to you."
  >
    You can only rename campaigns credited to you. Reload the page. If the campaign is still in **All Campaigns** and the error repeats, contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="The campaign has the wrong ad channel"
    description="The ad channel can't be changed after the link is created."
  >
    Create a new link from **All Campaigns → Create Link**. See [Create a campaign link](/affiliate-partners/create-campaign-link).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="The campaign is not in your list"
    description="You can only edit campaigns attributed to you."
  >
    The campaign belongs to the advertiser or another partner. Only campaigns that credit you appear in **All Campaigns**, and only those can be renamed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
