Create an affiliate account before you receive Linkrunner tracking links or postbacks.

<Info>
  You need a work email, a network logo, a short description, and a valid post-install event URL. The install postback URL and website are optional.
</Info>

## 1. Start your registration

Open [Affiliate Partner Sign Up](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/affiliate/signup).

Choose **Continue with Google** or enter your **Work Email**, accept the terms, then click **Continue**.

![Affiliate partner signup form](/images/affiliate-partners/create-account-signup.webp)

If you use a work email, open the sign-in link sent to your inbox. The link expires after 15 minutes.

## 2. Add your network details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Upload a PNG, JPG, or WebP file up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your network">
    Enter your affiliate network name and a short description of the traffic or services you provide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    Enter your website URL if you have one. This field is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Click **Continue** to add your callback URLs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Completed affiliate network details](/images/affiliate-partners/create-account-network-details.webp)

## 3. Add your postback URLs

Enter the endpoints where Linkrunner should send attributed conversions:

- **Install Postback URL:** Optional. Add it if you want a separate callback for installs.
- **Post-Install Event URL:** Required. Add the endpoint that receives signup, purchase, and other in-app event postbacks.

Use complete HTTPS URLs, including any credentials that Linkrunner should use. You can edit the URLs later from your affiliate settings.

![Affiliate postback URL setup](/images/affiliate-partners/create-account-postbacks-filled.webp)

Click **Complete setup**.

<Note>
  Linkrunner sets up your postbacks when your application is approved. They go live on their own if every macro in your URLs is supported. If a URL uses an unsupported macro, setup fails, and you fix the URL in **Settings → Postback URLs**. Saving it sets up your postbacks again. Installs, and signups once set up, are sent automatically. In-app events are sent only after they are mapped. See [Postbacks and Macros](/affiliate-partners/postbacks) for event mapping, supported macros, and IP allowlisting.
</Note>

## 4. Wait for approval

The **Pending verification** page confirms that Linkrunner received your application. You can close the page safely.

![Affiliate application pending verification](/images/affiliate-partners/create-account-pending.webp)

Linkrunner emails you when the workspace is approved.

### What happens after approval

- **Your postbacks are set up** from the URLs you submitted. They go live automatically if the URLs only use [supported macros](/affiliate-partners/postbacks).
- **You can sign in**, but you will not see a customer's project yet. A customer has to connect you first. You cannot connect yourself.
- **Share your partner code** with the customer so they can find you. Copy it from **Settings → Affiliate Details**. They connect you from **Integrations → Affiliate Partners**. See [Connect an affiliate partner](/affiliate-partners/connect-an-affiliate).
- **Once connected**, create a campaign link, choose your in-app events, and send a test click.

See [Getting started as an affiliate partner](/affiliate-partners/getting-started) for the full checklist.

## Troubleshooting

**The email sign-in link expired.**
Return to [Affiliate Partner Sign Up](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/affiliate/signup) and request a new link.

**Continue is disabled on the network details step.**
Upload a supported logo, enter an affiliate name, and add a description.

**Complete setup is disabled.**
Enter a valid HTTPS post-install event URL. The install postback URL can remain blank.

**You are approved but see no project.**
No customer has connected you yet. Ask the customer to connect you from their Integrations page, and send them your partner code. See [Connect an affiliate partner](/affiliate-partners/connect-an-affiliate).

**Your workspace still shows Pending verification.**
Your application is waiting for Linkrunner approval. You receive an email after the review is complete.
