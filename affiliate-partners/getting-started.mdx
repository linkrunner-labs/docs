Linkrunner is the Mobile Measurement Partner (MMP) for the apps you run traffic to. It records your clicks, credits installs and in-app events to them, and sends you a postback for each conversion.

This page lists what you get and what to do after your account is approved.

## What Linkrunner offers you

| Feature | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Affiliate dashboard** | Your own workspace for every advertiser connected to you: campaigns, postback URLs, event mappings and logs. | [Create an account](/affiliate-partners/create-account) |
| **Tracking links** | Campaign links generated with your click ID and passthrough parameters already in the template. | [Create a campaign link](/affiliate-partners/create-campaign-link) |
| **Server-to-server clicks** | Report clicks from your server for placements that send users straight to the store, such as OEM app-store icons and preloads. | [S2S clicks](/affiliate-partners/s2s-clicks) |
| **Server-to-server impressions** (optional) | Report ad impressions so installs from users who saw your ad, but didn't tap it, can be credited to you (view-through). | [S2S impressions](/affiliate-partners/s2s-impressions) |
| **Postbacks** | A postback for every install, signup and mapped in-app event credited to you, carrying your click ID and the macros you choose. | [Postbacks and macros](/affiliate-partners/postbacks) |
| **Event mapping** | Choose which in-app events reach you, rename them to your goal names, and include revenue. | [Event mapping](/affiliate-partners/event-mapping) |
| **Postback logs** | See every postback Linkrunner sent you and what your endpoint answered. | [Postback logs](/affiliate-partners/postback-logs) |
| **Rejection postbacks** | Get told when an advertiser's Fraud Control rejects a conversion, with the reason. | [Rejected conversions](/affiliate-partners/postbacks#rejected-conversions) |

## What you need to do after approval

Linkrunner approves your account, but you cannot run traffic until your postbacks are live and a customer has connected you. Work through these steps in order. Each one names who does it and how you know it is done. In your dashboard, **Get started** shows which of these steps are done.

| # | Step | Who | How you know it's done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account approved | Linkrunner | You get the approval email and can sign in. |
| 2 | Postback URLs added and live | You | Under **Settings → Postback URLs**, your URLs are saved and no setup error shows. |
| 3 | Connected to a customer | The customer | Their project appears in your project selector. |
| 4 | Create a campaign link | You | The link shows under **All Campaigns** in the customer's project. |
| 5 | Choose in-app events (optional) | You or the customer | The events you want are listed in **Event Mapping**. |
| 6 | Send a test click and install | You | The install postback shows in **Postback logs** with your click ID. |

<Warning>
**Approved but you see no project?**
Your account is approved, but no customer has connected you yet. You cannot connect yourself. The customer has to do it.

1. Ask the customer to open **Integrations → Affiliate Partners** in their dashboard and click **Connect** on your card.
2. Send them your **partner code**. You can copy it from **Settings → Affiliate Details**. They use it to find you.
3. Point them to [Connect an affiliate partner](/affiliate-partners/connect-an-affiliate), which walks them through it.

Once they connect you, their project appears in your project selector and you get an email.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for approval (Linkrunner)">
    Sign up, add your network details and postback URLs, and submit your workspace. Linkrunner emails you once it's approved. See [Create an affiliate account](/affiliate-partners/create-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your postback URLs are live (you)">
    After approval, Linkrunner sets up your postbacks from the URLs you submitted. They go live on their own if every macro in the URL is supported. If a URL uses a macro Linkrunner does not support, setup fails. Fix the URL in **Settings → Postback URLs**. Saving it sets up your postbacks again.

    Check your URLs against [Postbacks and macros](/affiliate-partners/postbacks), and see [Common mistakes](/affiliate-partners/postbacks#common-mistakes) for the ones we see most.

    **Done when:** your URLs are saved under **Settings → Postback URLs** and no setup error shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get connected to a customer (the customer)">
    The customer connects your workspace from their **Integrations** page. See the box above if you are approved but see no project.

    **Done when:** the customer's project appears in your project selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a campaign link (you)">
    In the customer's project, open **All Campaigns → Create Link** and copy the generated template. Don't build the URL by hand. See [Create a campaign link](/affiliate-partners/create-campaign-link).

    **Done when:** the campaign shows under **All Campaigns** with a link in the **Content** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the link to your platform (you)">
    Your platform must replace every macro, such as `{click_id}`, before the click reaches Linkrunner, and send a unique `tid` on every click. See [Tracking links](/affiliate-partners/tracking-links).

    If your platform uses different macro names from the template, tell Linkrunner what to send. See [What to send Linkrunner](#what-to-send-linkrunner) below.

    If your placement sends users straight to the store without opening a browser, report the click from your server instead. See [S2S clicks](/affiliate-partners/s2s-clicks).

    Optional: to get credit for users who see your ad but don't tap it, also report impressions. See [S2S impressions](/affiliate-partners/s2s-impressions).

    Every S2S click and impression must carry your partner token. Generate it once in [**Settings → S2S Credentials**](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/affiliate/settings?tab=s2s); it works for every app you're connected to. See [Authenticate your requests](/affiliate-partners/s2s-clicks#authenticate-your-requests).

    **Done when:** a test click reaches Linkrunner with real values instead of `{click_id}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose in-app events (you or the customer, optional)">
    Installs are sent automatically, and signups once a signup postback is set up. In-app events are sent only after they are mapped, so map the ones you want in [Event mapping](/affiliate-partners/event-mapping). The customer can map events too.

    If your endpoint filters incoming traffic, [allowlist Linkrunner's postback IPs](/affiliate-partners/postbacks#allowlist-linkrunner-postback-ips).

    **Done when:** the events you want are listed in **Event Mapping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test click and install (you)">
    Send a test click from a device, then install and open the app on it. In [Postback logs](/affiliate-partners/postback-logs), confirm the install postback reached your endpoint with your click ID.

    **Done when:** the install postback shows in Postback logs with your click ID and your endpoint answered with a success status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to send Linkrunner

The campaign link template uses the click macros Linkrunner has on file for your account. If your platform names them differently, send [support@linkrunner.io](mailto:support@linkrunner.io) the macro your platform uses for each value below. Do not edit the generated link by hand.

| Value | Linkrunner parameter | What to send us |
|---|---|---|
| Click ID | `tid` | Your platform's click ID macro, for example `{click_id}` |
| Campaign or source ID | `s2` | Your platform's campaign or source ID macro |
| Site, placement or sub-publisher ID | `s3` | Your platform's site ID macro |
| Another sub ID | `s4` | Any other value you want returned in postbacks |
| Android advertising ID | `gaid` | Your platform's GAID macro |
| iOS advertising ID | `idfa` | Your platform's IDFA macro |

Linkrunner returns `tid`, `s2`, `s3`, and `s4` in your postbacks as `{click_id}`, `{s2}`, `{s3}`, and `{s4}`. For example, to get your site ID back, send it as `s3` on the click and use `{s3}` in the postback URL. See [Tracking links](/affiliate-partners/tracking-links#parameters).

For how a click becomes a postback, see [How attribution works](/affiliate-partners/overview).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which conversions do I get by default?" icon="list-check">
    Installs. Signups arrive once a signup postback is set up for you. In-app events arrive only after they are mapped, by you or by the advertiser. See [Event mapping](/affiliate-partners/event-mapping).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I match a postback to my click?" icon="link">
    Linkrunner returns the `tid` you sent on the click in the `{click_id}` and `{tid}` macros. See [Postbacks and macros](/affiliate-partners/postbacks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reuse my AppsFlyer templates?" icon="arrows-rotate">
    For server-to-server clicks, yes. The S2S click API accepts the AppsFlyer parameter names. You change the host and `c`, and add your partner token. See [Coming from AppsFlyer](/affiliate-partners/s2s-clicks#coming-from-appsflyer).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to integrate an SDK?" icon="mobile">
    No. The advertiser's app already runs the Linkrunner SDK. You send clicks and receive postbacks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
