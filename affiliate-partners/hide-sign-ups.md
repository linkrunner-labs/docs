# Show or Hide Sign-ups for Partners

<p class="vp-lead">Choose whether each affiliate partner sees sign-ups on its own campaigns</p>

Affiliate partners see clicks and installs on the campaigns attributed to them. Whether they also see sign-ups is your choice, per partner. Hide them when you pay partners on your own sign-up count, so they don't work from a different number.

| Partner | Sign-ups |
| --- | --- |
| Already connected when this setting was added (November 2026) | Shown, until you hide them |
| Connected after that | Hidden, until you show them |

When sign-ups are hidden, the partner doesn't see:

- The **Sign-ups**, **Conversion** and **Cost Per Signup** columns on its campaigns, ad sets, creatives and keywords
- The **Sign Ups** figure, chart line and sign-up stage in the campaign details

It still sees clicks, installs and the [events you share](#share-events-with-a-partner). Postbacks don't change: if a partner receives sign-up postbacks today, it keeps receiving them.

<Info>

Only project admins and managers can change what partners see.

</Info>

## Show or hide sign-ups for one partner

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the partner">

Go to **Integrations → Affiliate Partners** and click **Configure** on the partner's card.

</Step>

<Step title="Turn Show sign-ups on or off">

Use the **Show sign-ups** switch at the top of the page. The change saves straight away.

![The Show sign-ups switch, turned off, on an affiliate partner's page](/images/affiliate-partners/hide-sign-ups-switch.webp)

</Step>

</Steps>

A partner with sign-ups hidden shows **Sign-ups hidden** next to its name on the **Affiliate Partners** cards.

## Show or hide sign-ups for all partners

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Access for all partners">

Go to **Integrations → Affiliate Partners** and click **Access for all partners**, next to the section title.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose Show or Hide">

Pick **Show** or **Hide** under **Sign-ups**. The dialog tells you how many partners change straight away.

![Access for all partners dialog with Hide selected and a note that 6 of 8 partners stop seeing sign-ups](/images/affiliate-partners/hide-sign-ups-all-partners.webp)

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm">

Click **Hide sign-ups from 8 partners** or **Show sign-ups to 8 partners**. The count is the number of partners connected to your project.

</Step>

</Steps>

This changes the partners connected now. Partners you connect later start with sign-ups hidden; show them from the partner's page.

<Tip>

Tell partners before you hide sign-ups. The column disappears from their dashboard the next time they load it.

</Tip>

## Share events with a partner

Separately from sign-ups, you choose which in-app events a partner sees as counts on its campaigns. On the partner's page, click **Share events** (or **Shared events** once some are shared), pick the events and click **Save**. Partners never see revenue, other partners' campaigns or your users.

If a partner needs a sign-up number that matches yours, share the event your app sends at registration instead of showing sign-ups.
