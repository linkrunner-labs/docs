## Overview

Linkrunner is a Mobile Measurement Partner (MMP). When you run traffic to an advertiser who uses Linkrunner, the integration works in two directions:

1. **You send us clicks.** You point your traffic at a Linkrunner tracking link and append your own identifiers (most importantly your click ID). Linkrunner records the click and uses it to attribute installs.
2. **We send you postbacks.** When an install or a post-install event is attributed to your click, Linkrunner fires a server-to-server postback to your endpoint with the data filled into the macros you define.

Optionally, you can also **send us impressions** (the ad was shown but not tapped), so Linkrunner can credit an install to your ad view when there is no click. See [View-through (optional)](#view-through-optional).

This is the same model used across the industry, so if you have integrated with other MMPs the flow will feel familiar.

<Note>
New affiliate partner? Start with [Getting Started](/affiliate-partners/getting-started) for the full setup checklist.
</Note>

```
Your traffic
   │  click  (you append tid + s2/s3/s4 params)
   ▼
Linkrunner tracking link  ──►  install attributed to your click
   │
   │  postback (we fill your macros: click_id, event_name, revenue, ...)
   ▼
Your endpoint
```

## How attribution works

The link between a click and a conversion is your **click ID**. You generate a unique ID for every click and pass it to Linkrunner on the tracking link as `tid`. When a conversion is attributed back to that click, Linkrunner returns the same value in the `{click_id}` and `{tid}` macros on the postback. You match the postback to your original click using that value.

<Note>
Pass a unique `tid` on every click. Without it, Linkrunner cannot echo an identifier back to you on the postback, and you cannot reconcile conversions against your clicks.
</Note>

### View-through (optional)

If you report impressions through [S2S impressions](/affiliate-partners/s2s-impressions), an install with no click can be credited to an impression instead:

- **Off by default.** The advertiser turns on view-through for your partner account and sets your window, from 1 to 24 hours.
- **Device ID only.** The impression must carry the installing device's `gaid` or `idfa`.
- **Clicks always win.** An impression is credited only when no click, from any source, can be credited.

The postback carries the `tid` you sent on the impression, and `{touch_type}` is `impression` instead of `click`, so you can tell view-through installs apart.

## What you can receive postbacks for

| Conversion | When it fires |
|---|---|
| **Install** | A new app install is attributed to your click, or to your impression if you use [view-through](#view-through-optional). Sent automatically. |
| **Signup** | The user completes registration, as defined by the advertiser. Sent automatically once a signup postback is set up. |
| **In-app event** | A post-install event the advertiser tracks (for example a purchase). Sent only for events that have an event mapping. |

In-app events are not sent by default. Linkrunner sends an in-app event only after it is mapped, either by the advertiser (**Integrations →** your network **→ Event Mapping**) or by you (**Event Mapping** in your affiliate dashboard). A mapping can rename the event to your goal name and can include revenue.

You provide one postback URL template per conversion you want to receive. See [Event mapping](/affiliate-partners/postbacks#event-mapping) for details.
