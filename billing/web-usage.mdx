Some billing accounts are billed on more than installs. A **web rate card** covers the users and events on a website tracked with the [Web SDK](/sdk/web), and your app rate card can also cover the events your app sends. Installs from your apps are billed as described in [Billing Overview](/billing/overview) and are not affected.

Each item below is billed only if it is set up on your account. Anything that is set up is billed in full: every number counted for the month is on your invoice.

## Web rate card

The web rate card can bill on tracked users, on web events, or on both. Your agreement decides which.

### Tracked users

The **tracked user** is one person who sent at least one accepted web event in the calendar month. Bot traffic is not counted.

- **Each person counts once per month**, however many pages they view or events they send.
- **Identified visitors count as one user.** When you call [`lr.identify`](/sdk/web#2-identify-users-and-track-events) for a visitor, that visitor and the identified user are the same person, not two.
- **Visitors you never identify count once each.**
- **Usage is added up across projects.** It is counted per project and summed across the projects on your billing account. A person who visits two of your projects in the same month counts once in each.

### Web events

Every accepted event your website sends counts once, and that includes pageviews, `identify` calls and custom events. Bot traffic is not counted. Web events are a different measure from tracked users: they grow with how much each person does, not with how many people visit.

Months follow your billing account's time zone.

## In-app events

In-app events are the events your app sends through the Linkrunner SDK, for example with `trackEvent`. Each event counts once in the calendar month. Payments and installs are not counted as in-app events. In-app events are priced on the same rate card as installs.

## Allowance and bands

Your rate card is on your [billing page](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/billing). For each item above it can include:

- **A monthly allowance.** It resets every month and is separate from the one-time free install pool in [Billing Overview](/billing/overview).
- **Bands of units, each with its own rate.** Only the units inside a band are charged at that band's rate. For example, if your first 25,000 users fall in a free band and the next 75,000 fall in a second band, only those 75,000 are charged at the second band's rate. This differs from installs, where one tier rate applies to every install.
- **A monthly platform fee, a minimum or maximum monthly charge, or a discount**, if your account has them.

Whatever applies to your account is listed on your rate card.

## When billing starts

Billing for each item starts on the date agreed with you. Nothing is invoiced for it before that date.

## Where you see it

Once an item is set up for your account:

- **Usage & Billing** shows it in the invoices table, in columns next to installs. The current month appears as an estimate row with the quantity so far. Project admins also see the allowance used, the billable units, the rate and the amount.
- **Your monthly invoice** is one invoice per month for the whole billing account, with the same schedule and payment options as installs. See [Billing Overview](/billing/overview).
