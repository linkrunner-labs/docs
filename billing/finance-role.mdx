The **Finance** role is for the people who pay your Linkrunner invoices, such as an accountant or finance manager. They can manage billing for a project, and they cannot see your campaigns, attribution data, users or settings.

## What a finance member can do

A finance member sees one page in the dashboard: **Usage & Billing**. On it, they can:

- View install usage for the project
- View and download invoices
- Pay invoices, by bank transfer or payment link
- Add or update the billing details, such as company name, address and GSTIN
- Use the [Linkrunner Wallet](/billing/wallet), if your account has it enabled

They also receive every billing email, along with your admins:

- A new invoice is ready
- Payment reminders and overdue notices
- Payment received, with the receipt
- A wallet recharge is approved
- Account access is restricted or restored
- Requests for GST details

## What a finance member cannot see

Every other page is hidden, including campaigns, attribution, users, events, integrations, members and project settings. If a finance member opens a link to one of these pages, the dashboard sends them back to **Usage & Billing**. The page tells them they have the Finance role and that a project admin can change their access.

## Add a finance member

Only project admins can add a finance member or change someone's role to Finance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to [Settings → Members](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/settings/members) and click **Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email and choose Finance">
    Enter one or more email addresses, set **Role** to **Finance**, and click **Add**.

    <img src="/images/billing/finance-role-invite.webp" alt="The Invite Members dialog with the Finance role selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="They sign in">
    They get an email invitation. When they sign in, they land on **Usage & Billing**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change an existing member to Finance, or back, pick the new role in the **Role** column on the same page.

## Good to know

- **Access is per project.** Add the finance member to each project they should manage billing for.
- **Invoices cover the whole billing account.** If several projects share one billing account, a finance member on any of those projects receives the billing emails for the account.
- **To remove access,** delete the member from **Settings → Members**.
