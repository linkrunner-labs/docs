Open [SDK Testing → Web SDK](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/dashboard/sdk-console/web-sdk-setup) in the dashboard. You need to be a project admin or member.

## 1. Register your test browser

<Steps>
<Step title="Install the Web SDK">
Choose how your website loads the SDK, copy the snippet, and deploy it to your website. Your token is already filled in.

![Install the Web SDK step with the install options and the snippet](/images/web-sdk-testing/register-install.webp)
</Step>

<Step title="Open the registration link">
Choose **Production**, or **Other URL** to test on a staging or local site. Then click **Open** to open the registration link in the browser you want to test with.

![Site selector set to Production and the registration link ending in lr_test](/images/web-sdk-testing/register-test-link.webp)
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm your browser">
Linkrunner registers your browser and moves to the next step. If more than one browser opened the link, click **Register** next to the one you are testing with.

![Test browser registered as Chrome on macOS](/images/web-sdk-testing/browser-registered.webp)
</Step>
</Steps>

The next steps only count events from this browser, so other visitors on your website do not affect the test.

## 2. Test a sign-up

Sign up on your website in your test browser. When the step shows **Sign-up received**, click **Next**.

![Sign-up Tracking step showing Sign-up received](/images/web-sdk-testing/signup-verified.webp)

Your website needs to call `identify` and send a `signup` event. The step shows the code for your setup, and [Identify users and track events](/sdk/web#2-identify-users-and-track-events) explains it.

## 3. Test campaign attribution

Copy the link under **Copy your test link** and open it in your test browser. The step shows **Visit attributed to Test Campaign**.

![Attribution Testing step showing Visit attributed to Test Campaign](/images/web-sdk-testing/attribution-verified.webp)

## 4. Test a custom event

Do something on your website that sends an event, such as adding an item to the cart. The step shows **Custom event received**.

![Custom Events Tracking step showing Custom event received](/images/web-sdk-testing/custom-event-verified.webp)

## 5. Test a purchase

Make a test purchase on your website. The step shows **Purchase received** with the amount.

![Revenue Event Tracking step showing Purchase received](/images/web-sdk-testing/revenue-verified.webp)

Click **Done**. **Web Campaigns** opens with a **Test Campaign** row for your test visit.

![Web Campaigns with the Test Campaign row highlighted](/images/web-sdk-testing/web-campaigns-test-campaign.webp)

## If something does not show up

- **Your browser does not appear after you open the registration link:** check that the snippet is live on the site you opened, and turn off ad blockers in your test browser.
- **A step keeps waiting:** send the event from the browser you registered, after the step has opened.
