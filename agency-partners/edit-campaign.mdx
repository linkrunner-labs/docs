You can change a campaign's name, deep link, link preview, redirects and link options from your agency workspace. The changes apply to the campaign straight away.

## Before you start

You need:

- **Campaigns → Edit** turned on for your agency. It is the same switch that lets you create links. See [Ask the customer to turn on link creation](/agency-partners/create-campaign-link#1-ask-the-customer-to-turn-on-link-creation).
- A campaign you can see in **All Campaigns**. By default that is only the campaigns your agency created. If the customer limited you to certain networks, it is only campaigns on those networks.

## 1. Open the campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's project">
    Sign in to [Linkrunner Dashboard](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io) and pick the customer's project from the project picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    Open **All Campaigns** and click the campaign row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    **Edit** is at the top right of the campaign panel.

    ![The campaign panel with the Edit button at the top right](/images/agency-partners/edit-campaign-open.webp)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Change the details

![The Edit campaign form for an agency, with the name, deep link and three collapsed sections](/images/agency-partners/edit-campaign-form.webp)

| Field | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Campaign Name** | The name you and the customer see. The link stays the same. |
| **Deeplink Destination** | The app screen people land on, even after they install. To remove it, replace the path with `/`. |
| **Link Preview** | The title, description and image shown when the link is shared in chat apps. |
| **Custom Redirection** | A web page for desktop, Android or iOS users, instead of the normal route. |
| **Advanced Options** | **Shorten link**, **Open in app**, custom store listings, and a custom channel for links with no ad channel. |

The form uses the same fields as Create Link. See [Create a trackable link](/features/create-trackable-link) for what each one does.

<Warning>
The deep link path is part of the campaign link. After you change **Deeplink Destination**, copy the updated link from the campaign panel before you share it again.
</Warning>

## 3. Save

Click **Update**. You see **Campaign updated successfully**.

## What you can't change

| Setting | Why |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Fixed after creation. Changing it would break every link already shared on it. **Advanced Options** shows it greyed out. |
| **Ad Channel** | Fixed after creation. To credit a different partner, create a new link. |
| Turning the link off, deleting the campaign | Only the customer's team, and only for links with no ad channel. A link credited to a partner can't be turned off or deleted. |

![Advanced Options open, with the domain greyed out and the link options below it](/images/agency-partners/edit-campaign-advanced.webp)

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion
    title="&quot;You can't make this change&quot;"
    description="The customer has not let your agency edit this campaign."
  >
    Either **Campaigns → Edit** is off for your agency, or the campaign is outside the networks or campaigns the customer shares with you. Ask the customer to check your agency's access in **Settings → Members**. See [Control agency access](/agency-partners/control-agency-access).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="The campaign is not in your list"
    description="It is outside what the customer shares with you."
  >
    By default you only see campaigns your agency created. Ask the customer to turn off **Only campaigns they built** if you need to edit their other campaigns.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="&quot;Deep Link Error&quot;"
    description="Linkrunner could not read the destination."
  >
    Enter only the path, for example `/offers/credit-card`, with no spaces. The domain in front of it is fixed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="You need to switch off or delete the campaign"
    description="Only the customer's team can do this."
  >
    You may see **Delete** or **Link status** in the form, but they don't work for an agency. Ask the customer to do it from their own dashboard. See [Deactivate a campaign link](/features/deactivate-campaign-link).

    A link with an ad channel, such as one built for an affiliate, can't be turned off or deleted by anyone. Stop sending traffic to it instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
