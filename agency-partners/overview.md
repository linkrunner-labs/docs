# Agency Partner Integration

<p class="vp-lead">Run campaigns for Linkrunner customers from an agency workspace, with your own team and your own campaign attribution</p>

## Overview

An **agency workspace** lets you build and measure campaigns inside a customer's Linkrunner project without being a member of their company account.

You get one workspace, no matter how many Linkrunner customers you work with. Each customer that engages you appears in it, and you switch between them from the project picker.

## How an engagement starts

There are two ways in:

- **Sign up yourself** at [Agency Partner Sign Up](https://dashboard.linkrunner.io/agency/signup). See [Create an agency account](/agency-partners/sign-up).
- **Accept a customer's invitation.** A customer invites you from **Settings → Members → Invite Agency**, and their email is your starting point. See [Set up your agency workspace](/agency-partners/accept-invitation).

```
You sign up, or a customer invites you
   │
   ▼
You set up your workspace       (name, logo, description, contact details)
   │
   ▼
Linkrunner reviews it           (you get an email either way)
   │
   ▼
A customer engages you          (from their agency list, or by inviting you)
   │
   ▼
Their project appears in your workspace
```

Once your workspace is approved, you appear in the agency list customers choose from. A customer who engages you after that skips the setup and review. Their project shows up in your existing workspace straight away.

## What you can see

Each customer decides what their engagement gives you, so the answer differs from one customer to the next. It ranges from the campaigns table on its own, narrowed to the campaigns your agency built, up to every screen across their whole project.

Campaigns you create are always attributed to your agency. Whether you also see the customer's own campaigns, and those of their other partners, is a setting they control.

A screen a customer has not granted you says so when you open it, rather than showing an empty page. Ask them to change it from **Settings → Members**, on your agency's row. See [Control agency access](/agency-partners/control-agency-access) for what they can choose.
