Engage an agency when you want someone outside your company to run campaigns in your Linkrunner project. They get a workspace of their own rather than a seat in your account, so you never share your team's access with them.

<Info>
Only project admins can invite an agency.
</Info>

## 1. Open the Members page

Open **Settings** (1), go to **Members** (2), then click **Invite Agency** (3).

![Settings, then Members, then the Invite Agency button](/images/agency-partners/members-invite-agency-button.png)

## 2. Pick an agency, or invite a new one

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the directory">
    The list holds every agency already set up on Linkrunner. Search by name, description, or website, tick one or more, then click **Invite agency**.

    An agency you have already engaged shows **Invited** or **Active** instead of a checkbox, so it cannot be invited twice.

    ![Choosing an agency from the directory](/images/agency-partners/invite-agency-directory.png)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By email">
    If your agency is not on Linkrunner yet, click **Invite them by email** at the bottom of the list, enter the address, then click **Send Invite**.

    Send it to whoever should own the workspace. They set it up and invite the rest of their team themselves.

    ![Inviting a new agency by email](/images/agency-partners/invite-agency-by-email.png)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From Integrations">
    The same agencies appear as cards under **Integrations → Agencies**. **Connect** (1) engages one. A card you have already engaged shows **Active** and offers **Disconnect** (2) instead.

    ![Connect (1) engages an agency, and an engaged one offers Disconnect (2)](/images/agency-partners/integrations-agencies-connect.png)

    Connecting here opens the access panel straight away, so you choose what the agency sees without leaving the page. **Invite Agency** at the top of the section sends an email invitation, exactly as the other tabs describe.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 3. Track the engagement

Agencies appear in the members table underneath your people, marked **Agency**.

![Members table showing an engaged agency](/images/agency-partners/members-agency-row.png)

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Agency · Invited** | Nobody at the agency has accepted yet, or their workspace is still being reviewed by Linkrunner. |
| **Agency** | Active. They have access to this project. |
| **Agency · Suspended** | Linkrunner has suspended the workspace. Their access is paused, and your engagement is kept. |

<Note>
An agency invited by email needs to complete its workspace setup and pass Linkrunner's review before it becomes active. A new agency will not have access on the day you invite it.
</Note>

## 4. Give them access

An active engagement grants nothing on its own. Click the sliders icon on the agency's row to choose the screens, actions, and data they reach. Until you do, they see an access message instead of your project.

Connecting an agency from **Integrations → Agencies** opens the same panel the moment the engagement is created. Either way the sliders icon on this page reopens it, so what you decided is never out of reach.

[Control agency access](/agency-partners/control-agency-access) covers each switch and what the agency sees when one is off.

## End an engagement

Click the remove icon on the agency's row, then **End Engagement**.

Everyone at that agency loses access to this project immediately. The campaigns they created stay with your project, along with all of their historical data. The agency's other customers are unaffected.

On the agency's card under **Integrations → Agencies**, **Disconnect** asks first whether you would rather narrow what they see. **Limit access** (1) opens the access panel, and **Disconnect** (2) ends the engagement as above.

![Limit access (1) opens the access panel, Disconnect (2) ends the engagement](/images/agency-partners/agency-disconnect-choice.png)

## Troubleshooting

**The Invite Agency button is missing.**
You need to be an admin on the project.

**"The agency list could not be loaded for this project".**
You are viewing the project as a partner rather than as an admin. Agencies are managed by a project admin.

**The invitation was refused as a duplicate.**
That address already belongs to an agency, or already has a pending invitation for this project. The error message names the company involved.

**The agency says they cannot see a screen.**
They only get what you have granted them, and a new engagement starts with everything off. Open **Settings → Members**, click the sliders icon on their row, and turn on what they need. See [Control agency access](/agency-partners/control-agency-access).
